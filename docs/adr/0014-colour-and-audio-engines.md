# ADR-0014 — Colour as formulas baked into one LUT per clip; audio as a measured FFmpeg chain

**Why.** Spec §11 asks for a colour *pipeline* — source detection, input transform, working space, exposure / white balance, shot matching, skin tones, creative grade, output transform — "not just Log → LUT → Rec.709", and extensible to Apple Log, S-Log and more. Spec §18 fixes the audio order: dialogue → noise reduction → EQ → compression → de-esser → loudness → music with ducking → SFX → final mix. Both must run on two shared CPU cores, and third-party LUTs and sound libraries bring licences.

**Impact.**
*Colour.* `grade/1` keeps the spec's own fields (`input_transform`, `exposure`, `temperature`, `contrast`, `saturation`, `creative_profile`) plus `tint`, `intensity` and human-readable `notes`. `synthcut_color` implements the curves from their publications (Apple Log white paper, Sony S-Log3 / S-Gamut3.Cine, BT.1886) and the steps in the space where each is physically meaningful: exposure and white balance in linear light, a filmic tone curve for scene-referred Log, contrast (a power S-curve pivoting on 18 % grey that keeps black and white fixed) and saturation on the display image, then a named creative look written as a formula. The whole chain is evaluated on a 33³ grid and baked into one `.cube` per clip; FFmpeg applies it with `lut3d` (tetrahedral). Measured on the VPS (20 s at 720p): decode + encode 7.5 s, + 3D LUT 18.6 s with two filter threads — the same cost as FFmpeg's own `eq` + `colortemperature`, while carrying any transform. Automatic grades come from frames measured *after* the input transform (Log judged as it will look), damped and clamped, with notes the owner can read; shot matching pulls every shot of a scene to one exposure target (the scene median kept in a band) and neutral white balance.
*Audio.* `synthcut_audio` measures the voice from the 16 kHz speech track and the VAD spans (speech level, noise floor between words, SNR, clipping) and writes `mix/1`: high-pass, `afftdn` noise reduction sized by the noise floor (stronger when the SNR is low), a gentle dialogue EQ, a compressor just above the speech level, a de-esser, and two-pass `loudnorm` to the platform target (social / YouTube −14 LUFS, podcast −16, broadcast −23 EBU R128). Music is ducked under the voice with `sidechaincompress` keyed by the voice. Tests measure FFmpeg's actual output: the loudness lands within 1 LU and music drops more than 6 dB while someone speaks.
*Preview.* Until the Color and Audio agents run inside plans (Phase 6), `render.enhance_preview` applies the automatic grade and mix to one clip's proxy in one FFmpeg pass and keeps before / after stills of the same frame.

**Alternatives.** Commercial or community LUT packs (licences, and a LUT cannot know the clip's exposure); FFmpeg's `colortemperature` / `eq` chain (fixed order, no Log input transforms); OpenColorIO with ACES configs (heavy dependency for what a few published formulas do); single-pass `loudnorm` (pumps; misses the target on short clips).

**Decision.** Formulas → one baked LUT per clip; measured voice → an FFmpeg chain with two-pass loudness and sidechain ducking.
